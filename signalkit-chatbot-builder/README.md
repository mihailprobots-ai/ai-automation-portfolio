# SignalKit AI - Embeddable Chatbot Builder

Clickable product prototype for an embeddable AI chatbot builder.

## Live Flow

The prototype shows a launch-ready SaaS experience:

- Landing page that sells the product and pushes onboarding.
- Builder workspace with onboarding, document upload, test chat, widget builder, and paywall.
- ChatGPT-like test interface for checking bot answers.
- Embeddable widget builder with copy/paste script snippet.
- Pricing/paywall tied to real gated value: sync, analytics, white label, lead capture.
- Written walkthrough inside the page for quick reviewer evaluation.

## Product Idea

SignalKit AI helps companies turn their documentation, policies, FAQs, and product knowledge into a support chatbot. The chatbot can be tested inside the app and embedded into any website as a small customer-facing widget.

## Reviewer Walkthrough

1. Open the live demo.
2. Click `Build a bot`.
3. Continue to `Docs` and add mock product documents.
4. Open `Test chat` and send a question.
5. Open `Widget`, customize theme/welcome text, and copy the embed snippet.
6. Open `Paywall` to see monetization logic.

## Scope

This is a frontend-only clickable MVP prototype. Backend functionality is intentionally mocked, according to the assignment requirements.
